## Top 10 Ideas Worth Reusing

| # | Idea | Source | Why it's worth stealing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Disambiguation FAQ at top** ("We are not Cursor Inc., not CURSOR Software AG, we are the community") | Germany | India will face confusion with the Indian software CURSOR products and people who think `cursor.com/in` is official Cursor India. Necessary. |
| 2 | **Monthly community Roundups** (a digest of the month with photos, project highlights, news) | Sri Lanka | Sustained content cadence. Aggregates social proof. Gives ambassadors something to point to between events. |
| 3 | **Featured Projects** with status badges (Live / Marketing / Education) and a submission pipeline | Sri Lanka, Trento | India's indie-hacker ecosystem is huge; surfacing builders' projects is a magnet. |
| 4 | **Interactive country map** with past + upcoming event markers | Germany | India is geographically massive. A map makes scale tangible. |
| 5 | **Per-recap photo counts** ("154 photos") and richer thumbnails | Calgary, Colombia | High signal of "this event was real and full". |
| 6 | **"Community Lead for [City]"** wording for ambassadors | Serbia | Crystal clear chapter assignment. India needs this from Day 1. |
| 7 | **Volunteer / co-organizer layer** below ambassadors | Sri Lanka | The path to ambassador starts here. Recognizing volunteers expands the leadership pipeline. |
| 8 | **Countdown timer** to next event | Kenya | Gentle urgency. Time-zone aware. |
| 9 | **Lazy-loaded calendar / heavy embeds** | Colombia | Performance + UX win, and few competitors do it. |
| 10 | **WhatsApp + Discord dual entry** (not Discord-only) | Sri Lanka | India is WhatsApp-first. Pretending otherwise loses 60%+ of potential members. |

## Top 10 Mistakes to Avoid

| # | Mistake | Seen on | Why it hurts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bro-y SaaS tone** ("AI-powered revolution!", "vibrant network!", flag emojis as branding) | cursor-italy.com | Directly conflicts with Cursor's calm, technical brand voice. |
| 2 | **Vanity stats** ("100% engaged & active", "100% AI-Powered", "1 city covered") | cursor-italy.com, Brazil BH/POA | Insults the reader's intelligence. Either back numbers with evidence or omit. |
| 3 | **Empty placeholder sections** (Upcoming with "no events" placeholder, empty Featured) | Trento, Bulgaria, Croatia | Site reads as inactive. Hide what's empty. |
| 4 | **Hyphenated / awkward domains** (`cursor-croatia.com`, `cursor-italy.com`) | Croatia, Italy | Hard to share verbally. India must own a clean apex (recommendation in next phase). |
| 5 | **Duplicate copy across multi-city deployments** (same stats, same paragraphs) | Brazil BH ↔ POA | Localization is the entire point — duplication defeats it. |
| 6 | **Emoji as section markers** (📅 🕐 📍 on every card) | Brazil BH, POA | Diverges from Cursor's restrained typography. Use icons (Lucide) or text. |
| 7 | **Flaky hosting** (503s, slow TTFB) | Austria during crawl | Community sites must always load. Use Vercel/CDN + status monitoring. |
| 8 | **Discord as only chat entry** | most sites | Discord has friction in India (KYC, mobile UX, employer firewalls). WhatsApp/Telegram parity needed. |
| 9 | **Reaction widgets stuck at 0** | Sri Lanka roundup cards | Hide engagement widgets until you have engagement. |
| 10 | **Direct-to-Luma with no on-site context** | most sites | Loses ownership of the canonical event URL, loses SEO, loses control of share imagery. Always have an on-site event page. |

Additional anti-patterns:
- Showing a list of cities with no chapter content behind them.
- "Become an Ambassador" links that only point to global Cursor, no local intake.
- No timezone labelling on event times.
- Single-locale sites that hard-code English-only without a path for vernacular contribution.
- Heavy hero animations that block first paint.

## Cross-Cutting Observations

1. **Universal gap: SEO.** Zero sites have visible structured data, dynamic OG, or sitemaps that we could verify. India will lead by default if we ship these.
2. **Universal gap: Owned email lists.** Every site relies on Luma for "subscribe". India should own its list.
3. **Universal gap: Event detail pages.** Only Colombia has anything close. Event pages are SEO gold.
4. **Universal gap: Accessibility.** No site declared a stated a11y baseline.
5. **The two best sites (Germany, Sri Lanka) departed from the template most.** This validates the recommendation to use the template as inspiration, not as a forced framework.
6. **South-Asian sites (Sri Lanka, Indonesia, Brazil) are doing more interesting things than European sites.** They embed local realities — WhatsApp, Instagram, vernacular, volunteer layers. India should lean into this lineage.
7. **Communities that name venues by name** (cursorthailand: Decaf, Sarnies; SLC: Alpha Coffee; Brazil: Localiza Labs; Belgium: Bar Bougie) create *partner value* — those venues will want to host again, and other venues will want to be named.
8. **Two-ambassador sites feel thin; 5+ ambassador sites feel real.** India should launch with at least 6 visible ambassadors.
9. **None of the 20 sites have a true student / campus track.** Cursor itself has the Campus Lead program and the `/students` page, but no community site picks it up. India should pioneer this.
10. **None of the 20 sites have a startup-founders track.** Given India's ecosystem, this is a clear unfilled niche.
