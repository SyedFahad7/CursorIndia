# 03 — Launch Ideas & Activation Formats

> Think beyond "meetup" → **create a moment**. Big AI companies don't win by hosting an event;
> they create something people **want to post about**. Inspired by OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity,
> Notion, Figma, Vercel, Stripe.

## Core ideas

1. **"India-First Launch" celebration**
   Don't call it *"Cursor Meetup Hyderabad."* Call it *"Cursor India Launch, Hyderabad."*
   Developers love feeling part of history. (OpenAI's ChatGPT Go signaled India mattered.)

2. **Launch in three cities simultaneously**
   Bangalore + Hyderabad + Delhi start within ~the same hour. Live photos, live tweets,
   live LinkedIn posts, city shout-outs → *"Cursor is everywhere today."*

3. **Limited-edition city merch**
   Not generic shirts — *Cursor Hyderabad / Bangalore / Delhi* or subtle city-inspired art.
   People collect **exclusive** merch, not generic logos.

4. **"Built in India" showcase**
   Not just employee demos. Invite a startup founder + a student + a senior engineer.
   5 minutes each, theme *"What I built using Cursor."* → authenticity.

5. **Live coding battle**
   Two devs, same prompt, 30 minutes, audience picks winner by applause.
   Or **Developer vs Cursor Agent**. Goes viral.

6. **Ship something during the event**
   45-min build challenge → top 5 demos → audience votes → swag + free credits.
   People leave with something they actually built.

7. **Cursor Clinic**
   People bring broken repos / slow apps / confusing codebases → Cursor engineers fix live.
   They remember getting *their own* problem solved.

8. **Founder & engineer AMA**
   No slides. How Cursor was built, scaling, models, roadmap, startup story. Often the most memorable.

9. **"We came to your city"**
   Visit engineering colleges, startup offices, coworking spaces. Film everything → recap video.

10. **Community passport**
    Attend Bangalore / Hyderabad / Delhi → unique sticker per city. Collect all three → exclusive merch.
    Turns the tour into a **collectible experience**.

11. **Hidden easter eggs**
    Mystery swag boxes, golden ticket under a chair, secret QR unlocking extra credits,
    exclusive wallpaper pack, India launch sticker pack.

12. **"State of AI Coding" opener**
    Don't open with product slides. Open with: how many devs use AI today, biggest mistakes,
    future of software engineering, why Cursor believes coding is changing → *then* demo.

## The campaign spine (if I were planning it)

Build the whole campaign around one message:

> **India isn't just getting a cheaper plan. India is where Cursor is investing first.**

Then execute:
- Exclusive India pricing announcement
- Billboards in major tech hubs
- Simultaneous community events in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Delhi
- City-specific merch
- Live demos of the latest agentic workflows
- Local builders showcasing what they built with Cursor
- Nationwide social challenge (share your setup / project)
- High-quality photography + recap videos that make it feel **bigger than three meetups**

> Companies that build lasting communities (Figma, Notion, Stripe, Vercel) consistently focus on
> giving developers **experiences they want to talk about**. Anthropic has been investing in India
> via developer-focused events too — same community-first approach.

**Final thought:** With PR + billboards + India-first pricing already in play, the launch doesn't
need dozens of activities. It needs **one memorable story**.
