Cursor India Buildathon

A ~1-week online build sprint, judged entirely internally (no public voting), where the shortlisted finalists are brought to the Bangalore flagship to demo live on stage — and the full prize giveaway happens there. The online buildathon is the campaign; Bangalore is the grand finale.

Decision: no public / community voting

Public voting is gameable — finalists rally friends to mass-upvote, so the loudest network wins, not the best build. We judge 100% internally (Cursor team + ambassadors, on a fixed rubric). Fairer, on-brand for a "serious builders" event, and keeps quality high.

The one hard constraint

Finalists need ~3 days minimum to book train travel before Aug 1, so finalists must be announced by ~Jul 29. The earlier we kick off (Jul 11–14), the safer the booking window. Roadshow runs in parallel: Jul 25 start · Jul 30 Hyderabad · Aug 1 Bangalore.

Bangalore is where the prizes happen

The other city events (Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad) exist mainly to push people to start building and enter the buildathon. The complete prize giveaway happens only at Bangalore: internally-picked finalists demo live, and winners are announced in the room. Same model as Algorand "Change the Game" — virtual hack, then finalists invited to an in-person Grandmaster Demo Day.

Why finalists demo live (not just winners)

  • Fills the Bangalore stage with real builders → more energy, more content.
  • The trip itself is a reward — "finalist flown to Bangalore" is prestige.
  • Live winner reveal = the viral moment (giant-cheque photo, applause).
  • Judging already done internally; stage demo confirms + adds theatre.

Cash pool is optional too

We don't need a big cash prize pool. A large credits giveaway (Cursor plan credits + partner credits) works just as well — arguably better for a dev audience. Options, all open: (a) modest cash for Top 3 + big credits, (b) credits-only, or (c) cash + credits + travel. Covering travel is itself a strong reward, so cash can stay small.

Money model — optional lever

Travel support (optional)

Flat ₹5,000 per finalist toward train travel to Bangalore — same per-person rate whether solo or team (team of 3 = ₹15,000). Reimbursed against tickets, trains-first.

Modest cost: 8 finalist projects ≈ 8–20 people ≈ ₹40k–1L total — far cheaper than flights, and the highest-goodwill spend in the budget.

Precedents for travel-covered finalists

  • Algorand flies track winners to an in-person finale to pitch on the main stage.
  • ETHGlobal offers travel credits + gas reimbursements; finalists demo on stage.
  • ETHIndia runs a travel-reimbursement policy for accepted hackers.
  • Cursor Vancouver's prize included a Web Summit ticket per team member.

Where registrations & submissions live — pick one, all viable

Devfolio (managed)

India-native. Best-in-class judging (rubric auto-normalization, judge rooms, sponsor judging) + free microsite. Least setup effort.

Devpost (reach)

Biggest global reach — what Bolt used for the "World's Largest Hackathon." Good if we want "largest" optics + easy discovery.

Our own website (custom)

Take registrations ourselves, fully branded and bespoke. We control the experience and can wire in agents to pre-screen submissions and assist judging later. More build effort, most flexibility. (Fahad can build this.)

Open choices for the team

  • Kickoff date — push for Jul 11–14 so travel booking is safe.
  • Prizes — podium only, or add category prizes (Background Agents / feature / student).
  • Cash vs credits vs travel mix — all on the table.
  • Platform — Devfolio / Devpost / our own custom site.
  • How many finalists fly in: 5 / 8 / 10 (recommend 8).

Risks & mitigations

  • Public-vote gaming → removed entirely; internal rubric judging only.
  • Booking crunch → announce finalists by ~Jul 29; kick off earlier if possible.
  • Finalist can't travel → keep a runner-up on standby; allow remote demo fallback.
  • Judging load → ambassador rooms + video-first + agents pre-screen + partner-judged tracks.
  • Clash with Delhi hack → buildathon is the umbrella; Delhi projects can enter it.
