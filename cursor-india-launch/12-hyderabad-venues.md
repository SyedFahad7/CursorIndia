# Hyderabad Venue Options — ~250 pax, open space + AC + catering

Research for a Cursor community event in Hyderabad, **excluding** T-Hub, T-Works, and Novotel/HICC.
Filters: capacity ~250, an open-air / lawn / rooftop component, air-conditioned hall(s), on-site or allowed catering, and a track record of professional / tech-community events.

> Context on where the big communities go: **DevFest / GDG Cloud Hyderabad → Novotel HICC**, **PyConf Hyderabad → T-Hub**. So the "next tier" of serious venues below are the hotels, convention spaces, and managed event/coworking spaces. Rental prices are almost always enquiry-based; per-plate F&B figures are indicative starting points from listing sites and should be confirmed.

---

## Top recommendations (best fit for a 250-pax launch: talks indoors + networking outdoors)

### 1. Cyber Conventions — Hitech City / Kondapur  ⭐ best "open + AC + catering" combo
- **Capacity:** Halls from 50–800 (10,000 sq ft hall divisible into 3); a 22,000 sq ft fully-AC hall paired with a **15,000 sq ft open lawn**.
- **Open space:** Yes — large lawn with buffet / activity setup.
- **AC:** Yes, fully AC halls. **Catering:** In-house, two modern kitchens.
- **Why:** Purpose-built for corporate events/product launches right in the IT corridor; easily does 250 with room to scale. Indoor stage + outdoor networking in one place.

### 2. The Sky – By Vaaraahi — Kokapet (near Financial District / Gachibowli)  ⭐ open-sky concept
- **Capacity:** Lawn 500 seated / 1000 floating · **Indoor AC Hall 200 seated / 350** · Poolside 150/250.
- **Open space:** Yes — open-sky lawn + poolside. **AC:** Indoor hall. **Catering:** In-house.
- **Why:** Natural indoor-talks + outdoor-networking split; modern, photogenic, good for an evening launch feel.

### 3. Hotel Daspalla — "The Forum", Hitech City / Jubilee Hills  ⭐ right-sized & corporate-favorite
- **Capacity:** The Forum = 3,280 sq ft, **250 seated / 300 standing**. (Bigger halls exist: D Convention ~2,000 cap, Devaraya 7,500 sq ft if you scale.)
- **Open space:** Overlooks the **Heritage Rock Gardens** (open-air garden pre-function view). **AC:** Yes. **Catering:** In-house, veg from ~₹750/plate.
- **Why:** Exactly your size, AV + WiFi built-in, heavily used for press meets, product launches & networking. Strong professional-but-not-stuffy option and more affordable than 5-stars.

## Premium hotel options (clean alternatives to Novotel)

### 4. Le Meridien — Gachibowli
- **Capacity:** Sapphire hall **500 seated / 1000 floating**; plus **open-air Poolside & Rooftop** (100–150 each).
- **AC:** Yes. **Catering:** In-house only (~₹2,000/plate starting). Parking ~125 cars.
- **Why:** True 5-star polish + an open rooftop/poolside for the networking half.

### 5. Radisson Hyderabad — HITEC City
- **Capacity:** Multiple halls; Summit-II theatre ~196, combine Summit/Crystal blocks for **250+**.
- **AC:** Yes. **Catering:** In-house. **Open space:** Limited (banquet-style).
- **Why:** Reliable, well-run, right in the tech belt; good if you want a straightforward conference setup.

### 6. The Environ Convention — Gachibowli
- **Capacity:** 11-acre property, multiple venues incl. a glass hall (300–500) + large lawns (up to ~2,000).
- **Open space:** Extensive lawns. **AC:** Glass/indoor halls. **Catering:** Available.
- **Why:** Grand, spacious, destination-style feel with lots of outdoor room; premium.

## Trendy rooftop / restaurant spaces (great for an evening mixer, ~200)

### 8. Amnesia Sky Bar & Fusion 9 — Rooftop, Inorbit Mall, Madhapur
- **Capacity:** **35–220** open-air rooftop with skyline views.
- **Open space:** Yes (rooftop). **AC:** Partial (open-air). **Catering:** In-house F&B (~₹1,000 veg/plate).
- **Why:** Best "cool community mixer" vibe near the tech hub; slightly under 250 but ideal for networking-heavy formats.

### 9. Medley — Banjara Hills / Jubilee Hills
- **Capacity:** Up to **200 seated / 300 standing**, rooftop restaurant.
- **Catering:** In-house (buffet/plated). In-house DJ.
- **Why:** Casual-premium party/mixer setting for corporate events.

## Managed event / coworking spaces (they handle setup + AV + catering)

- **IndiQube (Pearl / Alpine, Gachibowli):** Markets event spaces (~up to 250) with venue setup, technical support, and gourmet catering managed for you. Good "community + turnkey" middle ground.
- **91springboard / WeWork (Rajapushpa, Vaswani):** Startup-community event spaces; capacities vary — confirm the specific hall for 250.
- **The Executive Centre (Salarpuria Sattva Knowledge City):** Premium but small (≤50) — **not for 250**; noted only to rule out.

## Community-soul option — with an important caveat

### Lamakaan — Banjara Hills
- **What:** Iconic open-air cultural space (5,000 sq yards), indoor + outdoor seating, cafe; the heart of Hyderabad's grassroots community scene.
- **Cost:** Free / nominal (₹500–₹1,000).
- ⚠️ **Caveat for a Cursor launch:** Events must be **open to all** (no invite-only), **no corporate sponsorship allowed**, tickets capped (~₹100–200), strict low-volume/noise rules, limited parking. Culturally beautiful but its rules conflict with a branded/sponsored product launch. Consider only for an unbranded community-style side session, not the flagship.
