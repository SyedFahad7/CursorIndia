## Q: "Why do you think Bangalore should be last?"
> "Bangalore works best as the last stop because it's India's biggest tech hub, so it naturally
> creates more momentum and excitement for the tour. From what I've seen, that's how most companies
> structure their India roadshows.
>
> I've been involved in ~4–5 India tours with companies like Make.com, Supabase, Postman, and a
> few others, and we've always wrapped up in Bengaluru. I'm also helping with another India tour
> later this year following the same approach.
>
> It ends up feeling like the big finale where everyone comes together. If possible, it'd be great
> to have all the India ambassadors there too — that tends to amplify the launch a lot, both in
> attendance and community buzz. Even the recent MLH AI Roadshow had Bangalore as a major stop with
> that same energy."

  investing first."**

- **Build Stations / Experience Stations** over lecture-style events.
- **City-themed merch**, passports, earned swag, attendee testimonials, recap video.
- Consider **simultaneous multi-city** moment for the launch day.
