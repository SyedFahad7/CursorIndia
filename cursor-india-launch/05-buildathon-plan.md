# 05 — Buildathon + Roadshow Plan

> **See [11-buildathon-blueprint.md](./11-buildathon-blueprint.md)** for the researched version:
> real examples (Bolt, Supabase, FOSS United, VibeCon), the judging-at-scale solution, and 3
> concrete format options. This file is the earlier high-level thinking.

> Verdict: an **online, India-wide async buildathon** as the campaign umbrella, with the
> **roadshow cities as activation points**, is stronger than squeezing a single-city in-person
> hackathon. (My honest rating of this direction: **~8.5–9/10**, and I prefer it over a Delhi-only hackathon.)

## Why this format wins

- Everyone gets an **equal chance** to participate regardless of location.
- Each offline stop becomes an **activation point**, not its own mini-hackathon.
- Avoids **fragmenting submissions** across cities.
- Cursor team judges **one** competition, not five.
- Every city feeds the **same narrative**: *"Join the India Buildathon, come to your local roadshow."*
- We're **< 3 weeks out** — an in-person judged hackathon per city isn't realistic. Async solves the time crunch.

## The structure

### Online buildathon (the campaign)
- **Async**, open for **2–3 weeks** (don't force one weekend — Hadi & I agree here).
- **Open to everyone** (remote included).
- **Judged collectively** across all cities → one leaderboard.
- Final **online demo day** to show top demos + announce winners.
- Framing candidate: *"India's (World's) Largest Cursor Buildathon"* (Sanjeed's line).

### Offline roadshow events (the activation)
Baseline format (Sanjeed's) — but **make it experiential** so people don't leave after the talk:
1. **Cursor team talk** — pro tips, recent launches, agentic workflows.
2. **Q&A.**
3. **Build session** — but *structured*, not a vague "go build":
   - 30-min **"Build with Cursor" challenge**.
   - **Mentors** (Cursor engineers + ambassadors) walking around helping.
   - End with **3–5 lightning demos** from volunteers → gives the room energy.

Add on top (pick per city):
- Live coding challenge / **Repo Doctor** segment.
- **Cursor Experience Stations** (see [08-experience-stations.md](./08-experience-stations.md)).
- **Office hours** with Cursor engineers.
- **Swag quests / passport** challenges.
- Attendee **testimonial interviews** for content.

## Participation & prizes

- Keep the buildathon **open to everyone** (don't restrict to offline attendees).
- Reward attendance with **special prizes**, e.g.:
  - Best Hyderabad submission
  - Best Bengaluru submission
  - Best Delhi submission
  - Best first-time builder
- Prizes overall: exclusive merch, Cursor credits, **feature on Cursor's social channels**.

## Proposed timeline

| Phase | When | What |
|-------|------|------|
| Launch buildathon | Week 1 | Announce online; open submissions |
| Roadshow | Weeks 1–3 | City events; each promotes the buildathon |
| Bangalore finale | ~July 25 (or later) | Biggest event; ideally all ambassadors |
| Demo day + winners | Final week | Online; announce collective winners |

## Risk / thing to watch

An unstructured "build session" gets **awkward** — some code, many won't. Always give it structure
(timer + prompt + mentors + lightning demos). That's the difference between energy and dead air.
